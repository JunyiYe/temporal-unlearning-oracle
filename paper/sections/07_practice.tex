\section{Practice}\label{sec:practice}

``Licensee shall cease use of the Data'' under-specifies model
obligations. A contract should name three things. The first is the
membership level, because strictness changes both the surrendered
data and the measured memory (Table~\ref{tab:levels}). The second is
the comparison standard, which is equivalence to the retrained
counterfactual (Eq.~\ref{eq:compliance}) and not inability to
predict. The third is the audit granularity, which the era level can
support and the single record cannot (Section~\ref{sec:rq3}).

Three cautions complete the picture. Do not quantize after
approximate unlearning. At LLM scale, quantization restores up to
$83\%$ of what was unlearned, while exact retraining is
immune~\cite{zhang2025quantfail}. Read over-forgetting as evidence
of post-hoc treatment rather than thoroughness, because the
retrained counterfactual never over-forgets. And a model with no
alpha can still fail an audit, because memorization is a property of
fitting, not of skill.
